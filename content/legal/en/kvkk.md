This clarification text has been prepared pursuant to Article 10 of the Turkish **Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698** ("KVKK") to inform users of the **parolla** website ([www.parolla.app](https://www.parolla.app)) and mobile apps (the "Service") about the processing of their personal data.

## Data Controller

Your personal data is processed by **Selim Doyranlı** acting as data controller.
Contact: [parollaapp@gmail.com](mailto:parollaapp@gmail.com)

## Personal Data Processed

| Data category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Name and surname, username |
| Contact | E-mail address |
| Visual | Profile photo / avatar |
| User activity | Scores, statistics, created quizzes, reviews, drawings, profile bio |
| Transaction security | IP address, browser/device information, server logs, authentication tokens |
| Marketing (consent-based) | Interest data obtained via advertising cookies |

## Purposes of Processing

Your personal data is processed for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing your membership account
- Storing and displaying game scores, leaderboards and user content
- Keeping the Service secure; preventing cheating, abuse and inappropriate content
- Improving the Service and fixing errors
- Serving ads and — subject to your explicit consent — personalising them
- Fulfilling legal obligations

## Legal Grounds

Your data is processed based on the following legal grounds set out in Article 5 of the KVKK:

- **Establishment and performance of a contract** (Art. 5/2-c): managing your account and game data
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 5/2-f): ensuring security, improving the Service, serving non-personalised ads
- **Compliance with a legal obligation** (Art. 5/2-ç): statutory retention and notification duties
- **Explicit consent** (Art. 5/1): personalised ads and advertising cookies, and transfers of personal data abroad

## Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to **Google, Apple, Cloudflare and Vercel** for hosting, sign-in (authentication) and advertising services. As these providers' servers are located abroad (particularly in the United States), such transfers are carried out on the basis of your explicit consent or the other conditions set out in Article 9 of the KVKK. Where legally required, your data may be shared with competent public authorities.

## Collection Method

Your data is collected electronically and by automated means (cookies, server logs) when you register, sign in with your Google/Apple account, play games, create content and visit the Service.

## Retention Period

Your data is kept while your membership continues. **When you delete your account, your data is suspended for 90 days**; if you do not sign in again within that period, your account and all of its data are permanently deleted. Technical data such as logs is erased at the end of reasonable retention periods.

## Your Rights Under Article 11 of the KVKK

By applying to the data controller, everyone has the right to:

- Learn whether their personal data is processed,
- Request information if it has been processed,
- Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
- Know the third parties to whom the data is transferred, in Turkey or abroad,
- Request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,
- Request erasure or destruction of the data within the framework of Article 7 of the KVKK,
- Request that corrections/erasures be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
- Object to a result arising against them through analysis exclusively by automated systems,
- Claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing.

## Applications

You can send requests regarding the rights above to [parollaapp@gmail.com](mailto:parollaapp@gmail.com). Applications are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest. You can also delete your account directly using the "Delete My Account" option on the account settings page.
