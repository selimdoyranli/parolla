This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, shared and protected when you use the **parolla** website ([www.parolla.app](https://www.parolla.app)) and the parolla mobile apps (together, the "Service").

The Service is operated by **Selim Doyranlı** ("we"). You can reach us at [parollaapp@gmail.com](mailto:parollaapp@gmail.com).

## Data We Collect

### When you create an account

You can sign in to parolla with your Google or Apple account. The provider shares your **name, e-mail address and account identifier** with us. The **username, display name, profile photo/avatar and bio** you set on your profile are also stored with your account.

Creating an account is optional; most games can be played without signing in.

### When you use the Service

- **Game data:** your scores, statistics and leaderboard results
- **Content you create:** quizzes, questions, reviews, drawings and your profile details
- **Technical data:** server logs such as IP address, browser/device information and error records

### Data stored on your device

Preferences such as theme, sound settings and in-progress game state are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent to our servers. See the [Cookie Policy](/en/page/cookie-policy) for details.

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide the Service: managing your account, storing your scores and content, building leaderboards
- To improve the Service: detecting errors, analysing aggregate usage
- To keep the Service safe: preventing cheating, abuse and inappropriate content
- To show ads: the Service is free and funded by advertising (see below)

## Advertising

parolla shows ads through **Google AdSense**. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure and — with your consent — personalise ads.

- Users in the **European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland** are shown a consent screen (provided by Google, IAB TCF compliant) before their personal data is processed for advertising. You can change your choices at any time from that screen or via the "privacy and cookie settings" link on the page.
- In the **iOS app**, personalised ads are only shown if you grant Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, ads are served non-personalised.
- To learn how Google uses data: [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with service providers required to run the Service:

- **Google** (Google sign-in, advertising, Google Tag Manager)
- **Apple** (Apple sign-in)
- **Cloudflare and Vercel** (hosting and content delivery infrastructure)

These providers' servers may be located abroad (particularly in the United States). We may disclose data to competent authorities where legally required.

## How Long We Keep Your Data

Your data is kept while your account is active. **When you delete your account, your data is suspended for 90 days;** if you sign in again within that period your account is reactivated. After 90 days your account and all of its data are permanently deleted. Technical records such as server logs are kept for reasonable periods and then erased automatically.

## Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. Users in the EU/EEA can exercise their GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection) through the same contact channel. Users in Turkey can find detailed information on the [KVKK Clarification Text](/en/page/kvkk-clarification-text) page.

Send your requests to [parollaapp@gmail.com](mailto:parollaapp@gmail.com). You can also delete your account yourself from the account settings page.

## Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and suspect this has happened, please contact us.

## Security

We apply industry-standard measures (encrypted connections/HTTPS, access controls) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Links to Other Sites

The Service may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and recommend reviewing their policies.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page together with its update date.
