This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies (including local storage) are used on **parolla** ([www.parolla.app](https://www.parolla.app)).

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites you visit save to your browser. Similarly, **local storage (localStorage)** is a technology that lets data be stored in your browser. This policy covers both.

## Types of Cookies and Storage We Use

### 1. Strictly necessary cookies and storage

Required for the Service to work; they cannot be turned off.

- **Session data:** authentication tokens that keep you signed in after logging in with Google/Apple
- **Security and infrastructure:** security and load-balancing cookies set by our hosting providers (Cloudflare)

### 2. Preference data (local storage)

Kept in your browser's local storage to remember your experience; never sent to our servers.

- Theme preference (dark/light)
- Sound effect settings
- In-progress game state and daily game progress
- Language preference

### 3. Advertising cookies (third party)

parolla shows ads through **Google AdSense**. Google and its certified partners use cookies to serve ads, cap their frequency, measure them and — if you have consented — personalise them to your interests.

- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising cookies are used for personalisation only if you consent on the consent screen (provided by Google, IAB TCF compliant).
- Details about Google's advertising cookies: [policies.google.com/technologies/cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies)

### 4. Analytics and tag management

We use **Google Tag Manager** to understand usage and improve the Service. Data collected in this scope is used for aggregate (anonymous) statistics.

## How to Manage Cookies

- **Consent screen:** if you are in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, you can change your advertising choices on the consent screen shown on your first visit, or via the "privacy and cookie settings" link on the page.
- **Browser settings:** you can delete or block cookies from your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break features such as signing in.
- **Google ad settings:** manage Google's ad personalisation at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).
- **iOS app:** personalised ads are shown only if you grant Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission; you can change it under Settings → Privacy → Tracking on your device.
