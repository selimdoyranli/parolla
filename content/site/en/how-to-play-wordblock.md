**Wordblock** is parolla's Wordle-style word puzzle in Turkish: find the hidden 5-, 6- or 7-letter word in at most **6 tries**. If you've been looking for a way to play Wordle in Turkish, this is it.

## Rules

- Before starting you choose a word length: **5, 6 or 7 letters**.
- Every guess must be a valid Turkish word of that length.
- After each guess the letters are colored:
  - **Green:** right letter, right position
  - **Yellow:** the letter is in the word but elsewhere
  - **Gray:** the letter is not in the word
- Find the word within **6 tries** to win.

## Tips

- Start with a vowel-rich word to maximize early clues.
- Drop gray letters from later guesses; try yellow letters in new positions.
- The 6- and 7-letter modes give you more clue space, but the word pool gets tougher.

## Where to Play

Play Wordblock at [www.parolla.app/en/wordblock](https://www.parolla.app/en/wordblock) or in the iOS/Android apps. It's free.
