**Wordblock** is parolla's Wordle-style daily word puzzle: find the hidden 5-, 6- or 7-letter word in at most **6 tries**. A new word arrives every day for every board. It started as a way to play Wordle in Turkish, and the English word list is live too — so you can play it in either language.

## Rules

- Before starting you choose a word length: **5, 6 or 7 letters**.
- Every guess must be a valid word of that length in the language you are playing in.
- After each guess the letters are colored:
  - **Green:** right letter, right position
  - **Yellow:** the letter is in the word but elsewhere
  - **Gray:** the letter is not in the word
- Find the word within **6 tries** to win.
- Each board gets a **new word every day**, the same one for everybody playing that board.

## Turkish and English boards

Wordblock runs a separate word list, keyboard and leaderboard per language. On the English
site you guess English words on a QWERTY keyboard; on the Turkish site you guess Turkish
words on a keyboard carrying ç, ğ, ı, ö, ş and ü. Scores stay on the board they were played
on, so the Turkish and English rankings never mix.

## Leaderboard

Every board — 5, 6 and 7 letters — has its own leaderboard with daily, weekly, monthly and
season rankings. Fewer guesses and less time on the clock mean a better rank. You need a free
parolla account to appear on it.

## Tips

- Start with a vowel-rich word to maximize early clues.
- Drop gray letters from later guesses; try yellow letters in new positions.
- The 6- and 7-letter modes give you more clue space, but the word pool gets tougher.

## Where to Play

Play Wordblock at [www.parolla.app/en/wordblock](https://www.parolla.app/en/wordblock) or in
the iOS/Android apps. The leaderboards live at
[www.parolla.app/en/wordblock/leaderboard](https://www.parolla.app/en/wordblock/leaderboard).
It's free.
