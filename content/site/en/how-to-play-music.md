The **Music Quiz** is parolla's guess-the-song game: you listen to a clip and name the song that is playing. It's a free music quiz for anyone confident in their music knowledge or looking to compete with friends.

## Guess the Song Playing

- First you build the round: search and pick **up to three artists**, or choose one of the
  featured **playlists** (genres, decades, moods).
- Then pick a **mode**; it sets how long the song plays.
- The game plays a short clip from your selection. Your goal is to guess **which song** is playing, from the options shown.
- You have **30 seconds** each round. When time runs out, the right answer is revealed and the round counts as missed.
- At the end you see your score and the mode you played, and you can share the result.

## Modes

| Mode | How long the song plays |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | 5 seconds |
| Medium | 3 seconds (default) |
| Hard | 1 second |
| Impossible | 0.5 seconds |

## Tips

- Intros are the most distinctive part — focus on the first seconds.
- On Hard and Impossible a single note or vocal tone is enough; you can replay the clip.
- Not sure? Compare the rhythm and vocal character against the options.
