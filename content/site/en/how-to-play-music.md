**Music Mode** is parolla's music guessing game: you listen to a clip and try to guess which song is playing. It's a fun mode for anyone confident in their music knowledge or looking to compete with friends.

## Guess the Song

- First you build the round: search and pick **up to three artists**, or choose one of the
  featured **playlists** (genres, decades, moods).
- The game then plays you a short clip from that selection.
- Your goal is to guess **which song** is playing, from the options shown.
- Correct guesses add to your score; you can share your result at the end.
- Because you choose the artists, the quiz is as easy or as obscure as you make it.

## Tips

- Intros are the most distinctive part — focus on the first seconds.
- Not sure? Compare the rhythm and vocal character against the options.

## Where to Play

Open Music Mode at [www.parolla.app/en/music](https://www.parolla.app/en/music) and the Guess the Song game at [www.parolla.app/en/music/guess-the-song](https://www.parolla.app/en/music/guess-the-song). Free on web, iOS and Android.
