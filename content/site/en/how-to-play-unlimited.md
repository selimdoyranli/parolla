**Unlimited Mode** lets you play parolla's A-to-Z word game back to back, as many times as you like, instead of once a day. It follows the same rules as Daily Mode; the only difference is that you can start a fresh game the moment one ends.

## Rules

- Each question belongs to a letter and **the answer starts with that letter**.
- The time limit is **5 minutes per game**.
- You can **pass** questions; passed ones return at the end of the round.
- After each game you can share your result and instantly start a **new game**.

## Who Is It For?

- Players who finished the daily game and want more
- Anyone practicing to grow their vocabulary and trivia knowledge
- A warm-up before taking on Daily Mode

## Where to Play

Play Unlimited Mode at [www.parolla.app/en/unlimited](https://www.parolla.app/en/unlimited) or in the iOS/Android apps. It's free and no account is required.
