**Creator Mode** is where you build your own quizzes on parolla and solve the thousands of quizzes other players have made. It works for everyone — from teachers preparing classroom activities to friend groups making quizzes for each other.

## Quiz Formats

- **Classic (A-to-Z):** parolla's signature format; every answer starts with the letter you choose.
- **Multiple choice:** you provide options for each question and players pick the right one.
- **Flashcards:** a question-and-answer format that works like flipping cards.

## Creating a Quiz

1. Go to the [Create Quiz](https://www.parolla.app/en/create-quiz) page.
2. Pick a format, write your questions and answers.
3. Add a title and tags, then publish; your quiz gets its own shareable link.

You can edit or remove your quiz at any time. Spam, copyright-infringing and inappropriate content is removed by moderation.

## Solving Quizzes

Find player-created quizzes on the [Quizzes](https://www.parolla.app/en/quizzes) page and browse them by category and popularity. If someone sends you a quiz link, just open it and play.
