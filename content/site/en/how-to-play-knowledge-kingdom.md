**Knowledge Kingdom** is parolla's idle tycoon game: tap to earn gold, buy production buildings and grow your kingdom by increasing your income per second. It's a relaxed-pace mode, different from the word games.

## Rules

- Every **tap** earns you gold.
- Buildings you buy generate **automatic income per second**.
- **Upgrading** buildings multiplies their production.
- The game keeps producing while you're away; collect the accumulated gold when you return.

## Tips

- Early on, upgrading cheap buildings usually grows income faster than saving for one expensive building.
- Compare cost-to-income ratios before buying.

## Where to Play

Play Knowledge Kingdom at [www.parolla.app/en/knowledge-kingdom](https://www.parolla.app/en/knowledge-kingdom) or in the iOS/Android apps. It's free. The mode is not listed among the modes on the home page — open it from this link directly.
