**Tour Mode** is parolla's competitive mode: you solve word game rounds back to back, collect points and race other players on periodic leaderboards. It's built for players who love rankings.

## Rules

- Questions use the A-to-Z format; **answers start with the question's letter**.
- Correct answers in each round convert to points; rounds are played consecutively.
- Your points feed the **leaderboard**, with daily, weekly and monthly rankings.
- Joining the leaderboard requires signing in with a free parolla account.

## Tips

- Fast, accurate answers grow your score the most; use passes strategically.
- Periodic boards reset regularly; a new period is a fresh shot at the top.

## Where to Play

Play Tour Mode at [www.parolla.app/en/tour](https://www.parolla.app/en/tour) or in the iOS/Android apps. The leaderboard lives at [www.parolla.app/en/tour/leaderboard](https://www.parolla.app/en/tour/leaderboard).
