**Daily Mode** is parolla's classic game: an A-to-Z word quiz refreshed every day at midnight, with questions organized by the letters of the alphabet. If you enjoy the alphabet-quiz TV show format, this is a free version you can play every day on the web and mobile.

## Rules

- Each question belongs to a letter of the alphabet and **the answer starts with that letter**.
- The total time limit is **5 minutes**, shared across all questions.
- You can **pass** a question you don't know; passed questions come back at the end of the round.
- Questions refresh **every day at midnight**; everyone solves the same set that day.
- At the end you can share a result card showing your correct, wrong and passed counts.

## Tips

- Don't get stuck on a hard question — pass it and save your time for the rest.
- Watch your spelling; answers are compared to the text you type.
- Play every day to keep your streak; regular play grows your vocabulary fast.

## Where to Play

Play Daily Mode at [www.parolla.app/en/daily](https://www.parolla.app/en/daily) or in the iOS/Android apps. No account is required; create a free account to save scores and join leaderboards.
