faq:
  - q: "What is parolla?"
    a: "parolla is a free Turkish word and trivia game platform featuring a daily A-to-Z word quiz, a Wordle-style puzzle called Wordblock, a music guessing game, player-created quizzes and a tournament mode. It's playable on the web at www.parolla.app and via the iOS and Android apps."
  - q: "Is parolla free?"
    a: "Yes. Every parolla game mode is free on the web and in the mobile apps."
  - q: "Do I need an account to play?"
    a: "No, you can play without an account. Creating a free account lets you save scores, join leaderboards and create quizzes."
  - q: "When does the daily word game refresh?"
    a: "Daily Mode questions refresh every day at midnight; everyone solves the same set that day."
  - q: "Is there a Wordle in Turkish?"
    a: "Yes. Wordblock on parolla is a Wordle-style daily puzzle: find the hidden 5-, 6- or 7-letter word of the day in at most 6 tries. It has a Turkish word list and an English one, with separate boards and leaderboards for each language."
  - q: "How do I create my own quiz?"
    a: "In Creator Mode, open Create Quiz and pick a format: Question-Answer (classic), This or That, or Flashcards. Write your questions, publish, and your quiz gets a shareable link."
  - q: "Are there leaderboards?"
    a: "Yes. Daily Mode, Tour Mode and Wordblock each have leaderboards with daily, weekly, monthly and season rankings; Wordblock keeps a separate board per word length and per language. You need a free account to appear on them."
  - q: "Which platforms is parolla available on?"
    a: "parolla runs in the web browser at www.parolla.app, plus the iOS app on the App Store and the Android app on Google Play."
  - q: "Can I play in English?"
    a: "Partly. The English version of the site is at www.parolla.app/en and carries Wordblock (with an English word list), Creator Mode and Music Mode. Daily, Unlimited, Tour and Draw are built on Turkish questions and words, so they live on the Turkish site at www.parolla.app."
  - q: "Is there a time limit?"
    a: "A-to-Z games (Daily and Unlimited) have a 5-minute total time limit. You can pass a question you don't know and retry it at the end of the round."
  - q: "Who develops parolla?"
    a: "parolla is developed by Selim Doyranlı (selimdoyranli). See the About page for details."
  - q: "I found a bug or have a suggestion — where can I write?"
    a: "Use the Contact section in the in-app menu. You can also submit question suggestions via the Suggest a Question link in the menu."
